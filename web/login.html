Vigil

Your servers are already telling you
what is about to break.

Vigil logs in over SSH, reads, and leaves. It finds what is wrong now, what is exposed, what is unpatched — and what changed since yesterday that nobody meant to change.

Nothing is installed. No agent, no daemon, no cron entry. One SSH session per scan, and every command it runs is in the repository.
Read-only, and unprivileged. An ordinary login with no sudo is enough. Where a check needs more, the dashboard says so instead of quietly showing less.
Keys are sealed. Private keys are encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a key that is not in the database, and are never written to disk.
